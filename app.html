    Sources

    The list of laws comes from the Congress.gov public laws list through its API. Bill histories, sponsors, committees and Congressional Research Service summaries come from the GovInfo bill status files. Party splits come from the House Clerk and Senate roll-call records.

    Method

    Word counts measure the enrolled bill, the final text both chambers passed. Statutes at Large citations come from the printed slip laws; recent laws have not been printed yet. Days to enactment run from introduction to the date the law was signed.

    The five types of law and the short headlines for laws that only have a long official title were written for this explorer. Official titles appear on every law's page.
