      Sources

      The law list comes from the Congress.gov public laws list. Bill histories, sponsors, committees and Congressional Research Service summaries come from GovInfo. Party splits come from the House Clerk and the Senate.

      Method

      Word counts measure the enrolled bill. Statutes at Large citations come from the printed slip laws; recent laws have not been printed yet. Days to law run from introduction to signature. The five law types and the short headlines for laws with only a long official title were written for the Lawbook.
